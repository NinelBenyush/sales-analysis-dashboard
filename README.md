## 🧭 Overview  

**Sales & Revenue Dashboard** was created to solve a common business challenge — **analyzing sales data and extracting actionable insights**.  
For many businesses, it can be difficult to understand revenue trends, product performance, and customer behavior, which can lead to:

- Unclear revenue trends and missed opportunities  
- Inefficient inventory and product management  
- Lack of insights about customer segments  

**Sales & Revenue Dashboard** offers a **data-driven solution** that analyzes raw sales data to **calculate key KPIs**, **visualize revenue and product performance**, and **segment customers** using RFM analysis — helping businesses make **smarter, informed decisions**.
---

## 🌟 Key Benefits  

✅ Clean and structured sales dataset  
📊 Calculate essential KPIs (monthly revenue, revenue per product/category, customer insights)  
🎯 Identify top-performing products and categories  
💻 Interactive dashboard for easy exploration  
📈 Segment customers using RFM analysis for better marketing decisions  

---

## ⚙️ System Architecture  

### 🖥️ Frontend  
- **Streamlit** – Interactive dashboard and visualization  

### 🧠 Backend / Data Analysis  
- **Python** – Programming language  
- **Pandas** – Data cleaning, grouping, preprocessing  
- **NumPy** – Numerical operations  
- **Matplotlib & Plotly** – Data visualizations (line, bar, pie charts)  
- **Scikit-learn** – Optional for advanced analytics  

### 🗄️ Dataset  
- CSV / Excel files containing:  
  - Order IDs  
  - Customer IDs  
  - Product names & categories  
  - Quantity & price  
  - Total revenue  
  - Order dates  
  - Country (optional)  

---

## 📊 Features  

- Monthly revenue trends (line chart)  
- Revenue by category and product (bar charts)  
- Top 10 products by revenue  
- Customer segmentation with RFM analysis (pie chart)  
- Interactive dashboard with sidebar filters (year, category, country)  
- Full dataset exploration table
